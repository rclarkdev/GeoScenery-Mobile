Good to know

A few quick answers

Who can see my scene?

You choose public or private when creating or editing it. Private scenes aren't publicly searchable. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Do I have to add a location?

No. Location is optional when you create a scene.

Does GeoScenery track my visits?

No automatic visit logging. Save a place to your personal visits only when you choose, and remove it whenever you like. Ratings can include optional feedback.
